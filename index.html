How can it not cost me a penny?

Your customers pay a small service fee on online orders, or buy vouchers and memberships. That money covers our fee, so nothing comes out of your pocket.

No. We never take a cut of your food. The fee is paid by your customers, on top of their order.

We’ll check with your ordering provider for you, and start your Curry Club and voucher campaign in the meantime.
