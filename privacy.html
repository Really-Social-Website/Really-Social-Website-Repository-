Privacy policy

Last amended: 23 June 2024

Your privacy is important to us, and so is being transparent about how we collect, use and share information about you. This policy explains what information we collect about you, how we use it, how long we keep it, how we share it, how we store and secure it, how you can access and control it, and how to contact us with questions or feedback.

Really Social Media Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) takes privacy and security very seriously. This policy covers the information we collect about you when you use our product or services, when you browse www.reallysocial.media or otherwise interact with us. It should be read together with our terms, which together apply to your use of our service.

This policy also explains your choices about how we use information about you, including how you can object to certain uses and how you can access and update certain information. If you do not agree to this policy, do not access or use our service or interact with any other aspect of our business.

What information we collect about you

We collect information about you when you provide it to us, when you use our service and when other sources provide it to us, as described below.

  • Contact information, such as first and last name, email address, business address and phone number.
  • Billing information, such as card number and billing address.
  • Unique identifiers, such as email addresses, internal identifiers or passwords.
  • Login details, such as credentials for Instagram or TikTok.
  • Customer data, such as name, email address and phone numbers.
  • Information about your business, such as company name, business type and staff names.
  • Information about your mobile device (if using our app): system version, model, app version and push notification identifier.

As is true of most websites, we gather certain information automatically. This may include Internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet service provider (ISP), referring and exit pages, the files viewed on our site, operating system, date and time stamp, and clickstream data, which we use to analyse trends in the aggregate and administer the site.

Really Social and its partners use cookies or similar technologies to analyse trends, administer the website, track users’ movements around the website or mobile applications, and gather demographic information about our user base as a whole. You can control cookies in your browser, but if you disable them, some features of our website or services may not work.

Advertising cookies. With your consent, we use the Meta Pixel to measure how well our adverts work, for example when someone sends us an enquiry after seeing an ad. It only loads if you choose “Accept” in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page.

Information others provide to us. We may receive information from third parties that you have authorised to provide information to us.

How we use information we collect

Your personal information is used for billing, identification, authentication, service improvement, delivering the service, research and contact.

Customer data. The only customer data we receive is either collected directly through our adverts and landing pages, or shared with us from a system you use, such as your online table bookings system, ordering system or EPOS platform. We will only use your customer data to provide services to your customers if we have your explicit permission. Your customer data is only ever used for marketing communications sent from your business to your customers, and is never shared with any other restaurant.

How long we keep the information we collect

How long we keep information depends on the type of information. After that time, we delete or anonymise it or, if this isn’t possible (for example, because it is stored in backup archives), we store it securely and isolate it from any further use until deletion is possible.

Account information. We keep your account information for as long as your account is active, and for 6 months afterwards in case you decide to reactivate our service. We also keep some information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, support business operations, and develop and improve our services. Where we keep information for service improvement, we take steps to remove information that directly identifies you.

Customer data. All personal data we store about your customers is removed and deleted from our systems within 30 days of cancelling the service.

Marketing information. If you have chosen to receive marketing emails from us, we keep information about your marketing preferences for a reasonable time from when you last showed interest in our services, such as when you last opened one of our emails or stopped using our service.

How we share information we collect

We may share your information with third parties who provide services on our behalf. They are authorised to use your personal information and customer data only as necessary to provide these services to us, which may include payment processing, customer service, transactional communications, marketing communications, research and analysis, and cloud computing infrastructure.

How we store and secure information we collect

We follow generally accepted standards to protect the personal information submitted to us, both in transmission and once received. Access to private data is restricted to Really Social employees, contractors and agents who need it to operate or improve our service. All of them are bound by confidentiality agreements and may face discipline, including termination or criminal prosecution, if they fail to meet these obligations.

We may keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. The internet is not a completely secure medium, and although we do our best to protect your data, we cannot guarantee the security of data transmitted to our website or service. Any transmission is at your own risk.

Third parties. Where we use third parties to help provide our services, we make sure their security policies and confidentiality arrangements meet, as a minimum, the same requirements we set ourselves.

Passwords. If you have a password for our mobile and web application, you are responsible for keeping it confidential. Please don’t share it with anyone.

Card details. You need to provide card details to subscribe to our service. These are passed directly to our payment provider, Stripe.

How to access and control your information

You have the right to request a copy of the information we hold about you and your business, to object to our use of your information (including for marketing), to request deletion or restriction of your information, or to request it in a structured, digital format. You can access, correct or request deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe. In some circumstances we may be required by law to keep your personal information, or may need to keep it to continue providing a service.

Other important privacy information

Children. Our service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete it.

Your choice. You may sign up to receive emails, newsletters or other communications from us. To stop receiving them, use the “Unsubscribe” link in our emails or contact us.

Changes to this policy. We may change this policy from time to time. We’ll post any changes on this page and, if they are significant, we’ll tell you by email.

GDPR

Really Social is GDPR compliant and registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). The following describes how we protect your personal data and the personal data of your customers.

Your rights include the right to be informed, the right of access, the right to rectification, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to data portability, the right to object, and rights related to automated decision-making.

Key principles. Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly and transparently; collected for specific, explicit and legitimate purposes; adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary; kept no longer than necessary; and processed securely.

How we comply. Everyone working at Really Social understands GDPR and their responsibilities. We have completed a company-wide data audit documenting what data we hold, where it comes from and how it’s processed, and we keep it up to date. By signing up to our service, you enter into an agreement that gives us a legitimate basis to process your data and your customer data, in line with GDPR requirements.

Data requests. If you have an account with us, you can access, correct or request deletion of your personal data by emailing success@reallysocial.media. We aim to respond within 20 days, well within the 30 days GDPR requires.

Responsible disclosure

We take the security of our systems seriously. If you’ve discovered a security issue, please don’t share it publicly. Report it to us at success@reallysocial.media, including where the issue is, its potential impact, and the steps needed to reproduce it.

We ask researchers to avoid privacy violations, disruption to production systems and destruction of data; to test only reallysocial.media, app.reallysocial.media and our mobile apps; and to keep any vulnerability confidential until we’ve had 90 days to resolve it. Physical testing, social engineering, systems not listed here, UI and spelling issues, and denial-of-service testing are out of scope. Please don’t send us personal data or card holder data.

If you follow these guidelines, we won’t pursue legal action related to your research, we’ll confirm your report within 72 hours and work with you to resolve it quickly, and we’ll credit you if you’re the first to report an issue that leads to a fix.
